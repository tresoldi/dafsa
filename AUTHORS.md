Acknowledgements
----------------

The algorithms are not original to this library; `ARCHITECTURE.md` and the
[references section of the User Guide](https://dafsa.tresoldi.org/USER_GUIDE/#references)
cite them. In particular, the incremental construction is Daciuk's, the substring
indexes follow Blumer et al., and the weighted layer follows Mohri.

Version 1.0 was based on Steve Hanov's public-domain Python sketch of Daciuk's
algorithm. Version 2.0 shares no implementation with 1.0 and contains no
third-party code.
